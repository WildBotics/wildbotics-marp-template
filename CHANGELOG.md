## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-02

### Changed

- Acknowledgements: the funding acknowledgement, then the EU disclaimer word

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-29

### Added

- `acknowledgements` layout: partner logos, the stacked EU emblem with the
  funding statement, and the web, Instagram, Bluesky and YouTube channels.
- A picture for the title slide, `![hero](photo.jpg)`, cropped to the curved
  area.

### Changed

- Title slide: a larger curved picture area, a graded green field after the
  network's Google Slides deck, and the small EU emblem.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

First release.

### Added

- `wildbotics` Marp theme with layouts `title`, `title-inverted`, `section`,
  `blank`, `blank-logo` and the default, adapted from the Flight Lab template.
- Wordmark, mark and curved edge artwork, inlined by `scripts/build-theme.mjs`.
- `.tag` utility for module codes.
- Example deck on the WildBotics training programme, ending with the funding acknowledgement.

# What WildBotics is

- **12 doctoral candidates** in 12 host organisations
- **12 beneficiaries** and **9 associated partners** across Europe, Kenya and Greenland
- Funded by the EU **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions**, 1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2029
- Autonomous **aerial, ground and aquatic robots**, sensors and AI, for:
  - monitoring wildlife populations and ecosystems
  - collecting and interpreting samples in the field

> Boosting ecosystem conservation practice through innovation in robotics, sensors and AI.

# Your training at a glance

<div class="columns">
<div>

### Structure

- **ECTS** for network modules, towards the usual **30** your doctoral school requires
- Alongside courses at your home institution and elsewhere
- Recognised by each partner university's registry
- Planned in your **Personal Career Development Plan**

</div>
<div>

### Delivery

- Three **multi-activity events**: M9 at FBK, M18 at TU Delft, M30 at Salamanca
- **eLearning** in the first six months
- **Hackathons and field events** in Italy, Germany, Kenya and Greenland

</div>
</div>
